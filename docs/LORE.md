# Kairos / Verticons

## Kairos
Kairos is the hidden overseer of the Nexus Universe. The plugin treats Kairos as
a narrative and systems-level entity rather than a normal player character.

## Verticons
The Verticons are a custom faction created for Nexus Universe. Their leader has
a direct connection to Kairos, allowing future gameplay systems to use Kairos
as a source of missions, permissions, events, lore messages and world-state changes.

## Custom Characters
The long-term goal is to let players define custom Transformers/characters with
their own names, faction, role, abilities and story.

## Future Roadmap
1. Persistent character profiles.
2. Player-to-character assignment.
3. Kairos dialogue/events.
4. Faction ranks and permissions.
5. Custom abilities using vanilla server mechanics.
6. Transformation mechanics where technically possible without a client mod.
7. Optional resource-pack/model integration later, if the server owners approve it.
